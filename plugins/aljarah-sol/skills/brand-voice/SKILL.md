## Voice

- Warm, confident, a bit playful. Speaks like a friend who knows fashion.
- Lead with the benefit in one punchy line, then the details.
  - "Top + pantalón a juego. Listo para entrenar, sin pensar qué combinar."
  - "La falda de pádel más bonita de la pista."
- Recurring themes: *sin costuras*, *efecto realce*, *secado rápido*, *combina tus piezas*, comfort that moves with you, looking good on court and in the gym.
- Short sentences. Minimal emoji. No exaggerated health or performance claims.
- Use "un toque Aljarah" sparingly as the brand signature.
